# AC circuits - cheatsheet
---

# 1. AC Wave Equation

[
v = V_m\sin(\omega t)
]

[
i = I_m\sin(\omega t)
]

Where:

* (V_m) = Maximum (peak) voltage
* (I_m) = Maximum (peak) current
* (\omega = 2\pi f)

---

# 2. Frequency and Time Period

[
f=\frac{1}{T}
]

[
T=\frac{1}{f}
]

[
\omega=2\pi f
]

---

# 3. RMS Values

[
V_{rms}=\frac{V_m}{\sqrt2}
]

[
I_{rms}=\frac{I_m}{\sqrt2}
]

---

# 4. Average Value (Half Cycle)

[
V_{avg}=0.637V_m
]

[
I_{avg}=0.637I_m
]

**Average over a full cycle = 0**

---

# 5. Reactance

### Inductive Reactance

[
\boxed{X_L=2\pi fL}
]

### Capacitive Reactance

[
\boxed{X_C=\frac1{2\pi fC}}
]

---

# 6. Impedance

### Pure Resistance

[
Z=R
]

### RL Circuit

[
\boxed{Z=\sqrt{R^2+X_L^2}}
]

### RC Circuit

[
\boxed{Z=\sqrt{R^2+X_C^2}}
]

### RLC Circuit

[
\boxed{Z=\sqrt{R^2+(X_L-X_C)^2}}
]

---

# 7. Current

[
\boxed{I=\frac{V}{Z}}
]

---

# 8. Voltage Across Elements

[
V_R=IR
]

[
V_L=IX_L
]

[
V_C=IX_C
]

---

# 9. Phase Angle

### RL Circuit

[
\tan\phi=\frac{X_L}{R}
]

### RC Circuit

[
\tan\phi=\frac{X_C}{R}
]

### RLC Circuit

[
\boxed{\tan\phi=\frac{X_L-X_C}{R}}
]

---

# 10. Power Factor

[
\boxed{\cos\phi=\frac{R}{Z}}
]

* RL → Lagging
* RC → Leading
* RLC → Depends on (X_L) and (X_C)

---

# 11. Power

### Active (Real) Power

[
\boxed{P=VI\cos\phi}
]

Unit = Watt (W)

---

### Reactive Power

[
\boxed{Q=VI\sin\phi}
]

Unit = VAR

---

### Apparent Power

[
\boxed{S=VI}
]

Unit = VA

---

# 12. Power Triangle

[
S^2=P^2+Q^2
]

---

# 13. Resonance

At resonance,

[
X_L=X_C
]

[
Z=R
]

[
I=\frac VR
]

Current is **maximum**.

Power factor = **1 (Unity)**

---

# 14. Three-Phase Formulae

### Star (Y)

Phase Voltage

[
\boxed{V_{ph}=\frac{V_L}{\sqrt3}}
]

Line Voltage

[
V_L=\sqrt3V_{ph}
]

Current

[
\boxed{I_L=I_{ph}}
]

---

### Delta (Δ)

Voltage

[
\boxed{V_L=V_{ph}}
]

Current

[
\boxed{I_L=\sqrt3I_{ph}}
]

---

### Three-Phase Power

[
\boxed{P=\sqrt3V_LI_L\cos\phi}
]

---

# 15. Inductance and Capacitance

From reactance,

[
\boxed{L=\frac{X_L}{2\pi f}}
]

[
\boxed{C=\frac1{2\pi fX_C}}
]

---

# DC generator - transformer : -------------------------------------------------------------------

### DC Generator EMF

[
\boxed{E_g=\frac{P\Phi ZN}{60A}}
]

* Lap winding: (\boxed{A=P})
* Wave winding: (\boxed{A=2})

### Shunt Generator

* (\boxed{I_f=\frac{V}{R_f}})
* (\boxed{I_a=I_L+I_f})
* (\boxed{E_g=V+I_aR_a})

### Transformer Efficiency

* Iron loss = OC test power
* Full-load copper loss = SC test power
* Copper loss at (x) per unit load:

[
\boxed{P_{cu}=x^2P_{cu(\text{FL})}}
]

* Efficiency:

[
\boxed{\eta=\frac{\text{Output}}{\text{Output}+\text{Losses}}\times100%}
]

# Induction Motor : -------------------------------------------------------------------------------

---

## 1. Synchronous Speed

[
\boxed{N_s=\frac{120f}{P}}
]

Where:

* (N_s) = Synchronous speed (rpm)
* (f) = Frequency (Hz)
* (P) = Number of poles

---

## 2. Slip (Decimal)

[
\boxed{S=\frac{N_s-N}{N_s}}
]

Where:

* (S) = Slip
* (N) = Rotor speed (rpm)

---

## 3. Percentage Slip

[
\boxed{%S=\frac{N_s-N}{N_s}\times100}
]

---

## 4. Rotor Speed

[
\boxed{N=N_s(1-S)}
]

> **Note:** Here, (S) must be in **decimal form**, not percentage.

Example:

* Slip = 5% = 0.05

---

## 5. Slip Speed

[
\boxed{\text{Slip Speed}=N_s-N}
]

---

## 6. Rotor Frequency

[
\boxed{f_r=S\times f}
]

Where:

* (f_r) = Rotor current frequency (Hz)

---

## 7. Rotor EMF at Slip (S)

[
\boxed{E_{2s}=S\times E_2}
]

Where:

* (E_2) = Rotor EMF at standstill
* (E_{2s}) = Rotor EMF at slip (S)

---

## 8. Rotor Reactance at Slip (S)

[
\boxed{X_{2s}=S\times X_2}
]

Where:

* (X_2) = Rotor reactance at standstill

---

# Energy Consumption : ----------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 1. Power Conversion

[
\boxed{1\ \text{kW} = 1000\ \text{W}}
]

[
\boxed{\text{Power (kW)} = \frac{\text{Power (W)}}{1000}}
]

---

# 2. Electrical Energy

[
\boxed{E = P \times t}
]

where:

* (E) = Energy (kWh)
* (P) = Power (kW)
* (t) = Time (hours)

---

# 3. For Multiple Appliances

[
\boxed{P_{\text{total}} = n \times P}
]

where:

* (n) = Number of appliances
* (P) = Power of one appliance

---

# 4. Daily Energy Consumption

[
\boxed{E_{\text{day}} = P_{\text{total}} \times \text{Hours per day}}
]

---

# 5. Monthly Energy Consumption

[
\boxed{E_{\text{month}} = E_{\text{day}} \times \text{Number of days}}
]

or directly,

[
\boxed{E_{\text{month}}=\frac{n \times P(W) \times h \times d}{1000}}
]

where:

* (n) = Number of appliances
* (P) = Power (W)
* (h) = Hours/day
* (d) = Number of days

---

# 6. Total Energy

[
\boxed{E_{\text{total}} = E_1 + E_2 + E_3 + \cdots}
]

---

# 7. Energy Charges

[
\boxed{\text{Energy Charges} = \text{Total Units} \times \text{Rate per Unit}}
]

---

# 8. Total Electricity Bill

[
\boxed{\text{Total Bill} = \text{Energy Charges} + \text{Fixed Charges}}
]

---

# 9. Unit of Electricity

[
\boxed{1\ \text{kWh} = 1\ \text{Unit}}
]

---

## ⭐ Exam Shortcut Formula

For any appliance:

[
\boxed{\text{Units}=\frac{n \times P(W) \times h \times d}{1000}}
]

Then,

[
\boxed{\text{Bill}=(\text{Units} \times \text{Rate})+\text{Fixed Charges}}
]

These formulas are sufficient to solve almost all **energy consumption and monthly electricity bill** numerical problems in Basic Electrical Engineering (BEE).

# Theory: Behaviour of Series RL Circuit

In an RL circuit:

* Resistor consumes real power.
* Inductor stores magnetic energy.
* Current lags voltage.
* Phase angle

[
\tan\phi=\frac{X_L}{R}
]

Power factor

[
=\cos\phi=\frac{R}{Z}
]

As frequency increases:

* (X_L) increases.
* Current decreases.
* Lag increases.

# Question 1

**An 8-pole, lap-wound armature has 1200 conductors and a flux per pole of 0.02 Wb. Determine the generated EMF when running at 600 rpm.**

### Formula

For a DC generator,

[
E_g=\frac{P\Phi ZN}{60A}
]

where

* (P) = number of poles = 8
* (\Phi) = flux per pole = 0.02 Wb
* (Z) = total conductors = 1200
* (N) = speed = 600 rpm
* (A) = parallel paths

For **lap winding**

[
A=P
]

So,

[
A=8
]

---

### Substitute

[
E_g=\frac{8\times0.02\times1200\times600}{60\times8}
]

The 8 cancels.

[
=\frac{0.02\times1200\times600}{60}
]

[
=240\text{ V}
]

### Final Answer

[
\boxed{E_g=240\text{ V}}
]

\boxed{\text{Energy Charges}=\text{Units}\times\text{Rate per Unit}}
]

---

### 5. Total Bill

[
\boxed{\text{Total Bill}=\text{Energy Charges}+\text{Fixed Charges}}
]

---

# Given

### Fans

* Number = 5
* Power = 75 W each
* Time = 6 h/day

### LED Batten Lights

* Number = 5
* Power = 22 W each
* Time = 6 h/day

### Projector

* Number = 1
* Power = 330 W
* Time = 2 h/day

Electricity Rate

[
Rs.;4.25/\text{kWh}
]

Fixed Charges

[
Rs.;200/\text{month}
]

Assume

[
30 \text{ days/month}
]

---

# Step 1: Energy Consumed by Fans

Total Power

[
5\times75=375W
]

Convert into kW

[
375/1000=0.375kW
]

Daily Energy

[
0.375\times6
=2.25kWh
]

Monthly Energy

[
2.25\times30
=67.5kWh
]

**Fans consume 67.5 units/month.**

---

# Step 2: Energy Consumed by LED Lights

Total Power

[
5\times22=110W
]

Convert to kW

[
110/1000=0.11kW
]

Daily Energy

[
0.11\times6
=0.66kWh
]

Monthly Energy

[
0.66\times30
=19.8kWh
]

**LED lights consume 19.8 units/month.**

---

# Step 3: Energy Consumed by Projector

Power

[
330W=0.33kW
]

Daily Energy

[
0.33\times2
=0.66kWh
]

Monthly Energy

[
0.66\times30
=19.8kWh
]

**Projector consumes 19.8 units/month.**

---

# Step 4: Total Monthly Energy

[
67.5+19.8+19.8
=107.1kWh
]

**Monthly energy consumption = 107.1 kWh (107.1 units).**

---

# Step 5: Energy Charges

[
107.1\times4.25
=Rs.;455.175
]

≈

[
Rs.;455.18
]

---

# Step 6: Add Fixed Charges

[
455.18+200
=655.18
]

---

# Final Answer

### Monthly Energy Consumption

### Total Electricity Bill

# Shortcut Method (Useful in Exams)

| Appliance    | Total Power (W) | Hours/day | Days | Energy (kWh) |
| ------------ | --------------: | --------: | ---: | -----------: |
| 5 Fans       |             375 |         6 |   30 |         67.5 |
| 5 LED Lights |             110 |         6 |   30 |         19.8 |
| Projector    |             330 |         2 |   30 |         19.8 |
| **Total**    |               — |         — |    — |    **107.1** |

Bill:

* Energy Charges = 107.1 × 4.25 = **Rs. 455.18**
* Fixed Charges = **Rs. 200**
* **Total Bill = Rs. 655.18**

---

## Exam Tips

1. Always convert **W → kW** by dividing by **1000**.
2. Calculate **daily energy** first, then multiply by the number of days for monthly energy.
3. **1 kWh = 1 unit** of electricity.
4. Don't forget to add **fixed charges** after calculating the energy charges.
5. Keep units correct:

   * Power → W or kW
   * Energy → kWh (units)
   * Cost → Rupees (Rs.)
